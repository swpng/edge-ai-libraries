# Video Search and Summarization (VSS)

Transform your video content into searchable, actionable insights with AI-powered analysis.

## What Does VSS Do?

### 🔍 **Video Search**
Ask questions about your videos in natural language. Find specific scenes, events, or moments instantly across large video collections.

### 📝 **Video Summarization** 
Get concise summaries of long videos automatically. Extract key moments and insights without watching hours of content.

### 🔗 **Smart Combined Mode**
Search through AI-generated summaries for faster, more accurate results with fewer false matches.

## Why Use VSS?

- **Edge Processing**: Your data stays private with local processing
- **Natural Language**: Search using everyday questions, not technical queries
- **Multi-Modal Intelligence**: Analyzes video, audio, and text together
- **Enterprise Ready**: Scales from single videos to large collections

## Quick Start

1. **Deploy**: Set up the microservices
2. **Upload**: Add your video files
3. **Search**: Ask questions in natural language
4. **Results**: Get relevant clips and summaries
